Hi! I am Yan-Han Chen, currently a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the [Department of Aerospace Engineering at Iowa State University](https://www.aere.iastate.edu), where I work with Prof. [Abhishek Halder](https://abhishekhalder.org).

I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from Iowa State University, where I was fortunate to work with my (co)advisors Prof. [Hailiang Liu](https://faculty.sites.iastate.edu/hliu/), Prof. [Ruoyu Wu](https://sites.google.com/site/ruoyuwu90/), and Prof. [Arka P. Ghosh](https://sites.google.com/view/apghosh/).

**Research interests:** Schrödinger bridges, optimal transport, interacting particle systems, and machine learning.
