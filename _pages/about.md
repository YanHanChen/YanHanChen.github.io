Hi! I am Yan-Han Chen, currently a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the [Department of Aerospace Engineering at Iowa State University](https://www.aere.iastate.edu), where I work with Prof. [Abhishek Halder](https://abhishekhalder.org).

Prior to that, I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from Iowa State University, where I was fortunate to work with my (co)advisors Prof. [Hailiang Liu](https://faculty.sites.iastate.edu/hliu/), Prof. [Ruoyu Wu](https://sites.google.com/site/ruoyuwu90/), and Prof. [Arka P. Ghosh](https://sites.google.com/view/apghosh/).

**Research interests:**  Motivated by the need to develop rigorous mathematical theory and scalable data-driven methods for stochastic systems arising in modern large-scale applications, my current research interests span across Schrödinger Bridges, Interacting Particle Systems, Optimal Transport, and their intersection with machine learning.
